What it checks

A quick first pass,
not a clinical verdict.

Object inventory

Counts CT, MR and radiotherapy SOP classes before you open a dataset elsewhere.

Privacy signals

Flags populated patient name, ID, birth date and institution fields. It does not certify anonymization.

Reference consistency

Compares Study, Series and Frame of Reference identifiers exposed in the parsed headers.

Readable structure

Reports malformed or unsupported files without attempting to execute embedded content.
