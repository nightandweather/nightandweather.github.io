Make the photograph repeatable

The phone stays fixed while a 280 mm platform rotates to known angles. An ESP32 controls the motor, lighting, home sensor, and scale; an iPhone app coordinates an eight-view capture sequence over Bluetooth Low Energy.

More than a concept

The repository includes parametric CAD, twelve STL parts, dimensioned drawings, firmware, BLE communication code, wiring diagrams, a BOM, and assembly, calibration, and QA documents.
