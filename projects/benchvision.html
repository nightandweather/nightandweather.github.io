Two focused tools

SellShot measures sharpness, exposure, contrast, and resolution across a set of product photos to produce a repeatable ranking. PackCheck compares a reference image with a packing image, highlights changed regions, and exports an inspection record.

Local first

Photos of personal spaces and sale items never leave the browser. Analysis runs on the user's device, and even the demonstration images are generated locally.

Limits

The tools do not guarantee aesthetic quality, product authenticity, or packaging integrity. They provide repeatable measurements and direct a human reviewer to the places worth checking.
